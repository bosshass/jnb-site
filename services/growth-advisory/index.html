You have revenue. So why doesn't it feel like you're making money?

That question is where JNB does its best work. We use the data already sitting in QuickBooks to show you what is driving profit, what is draining it, and what decisions will actually move the business forward.

Let's look at the numbers

Bookkeeping tells us what happened. We use it to decide what happens next.

The numbers become useful when they change a decision: pricing, staffing, margins, overhead, purchasing, cash reserves, service mix, or the pace of growth.

Questions the data can answer

Why are sales up but cash is still tight?

Separate timing problems from margin problems, overhead growth, AR drag, owner distributions, debt, and real operating losses.

Can I afford to hire?

Model the fully loaded cost, required additional revenue, utilization, capacity, and cash impact before adding payroll.

Am I charging enough?

Use labor, materials, overhead, utilization, and target margin to see whether pricing actually supports the business you are trying to build.

Which work is actually profitable?

Compare customers, jobs, services, and revenue streams so growth is directed toward what produces contribution rather than just activity.

Where is the money going?

Turn a P&L from a tax-time document into an operating tool that identifies cost pressure, leakage, and changing trends early.

What should I do next?

Translate the analysis into a short list of decisions and measurable targets instead of handing you another report to interpret alone.
