What goes into the build

Designed for the customer. Built for the business.

Before we open a design file, we look at the services you sell, the questions customers ask, the actions you want visitors to take, and what makes your business different. The site structure, wording, and visual design come from that—not the other way around.

Design & customer experience

A clear page hierarchy, navigation, typography, colors, imagery, service storytelling, and strategically placed calls to action. Layouts are designed for desktop and mobile—not simply squeezed onto a phone.

Development & functionality

Custom page builds, responsive menus, service pages, contact/request forms, functional buttons and links, and agreed integrations such as scheduling or customer request tools.

SEO & measurement foundations

Page titles, descriptions, heading structure, internal links, speed-conscious design, and analytics/Search Console setup where access is provided. Tracking helps identify what works after launch.

Our process

What working together actually looks like

1

Discovery & direction

We define your audience, goals, service areas, conversion actions, existing content, and technical requirements. We identify what the site needs to accomplish.

2

Sitemap & page planning

We map the pages and menus, organize services, plan the visitor journey, and outline the content, imagery, and forms needed before development begins.

3

Visual design

We establish the look and feel: brand styling, page layouts, sections, buttons, mobile treatment, and clear paths to contact or request service.

4

Build & connect

We develop the actual pages, make the design responsive, configure forms and approved integrations, and prepare hosting and domain connections.

5

Review & quality checks

We review copy and layout, confirm links and form delivery, test key screen sizes, and work through the agreed review and revision process.

6

Launch & handoff

We publish, verify essential tracking and contact paths, and explain what happens next. Ongoing content, SEO, ads, and maintenance can be scoped separately.
