Monthly bookkeeping questions

Do you only work with businesses in Denver or Littleton?

No. JNB is based in Littleton and serves the Denver area as well as businesses nationwide, with remote QuickBooks and financial reporting workflows.

What if my books are already behind?

We may recommend a separate cleanup or catch-up project before recurring monthly bookkeeping. That way your ongoing reports start from trustworthy numbers.

Can you help me understand profit by job or customer?

Yes. Job costing and profitability review can be included when your bookkeeping and operational data support it. We will discuss the additional scope during discovery.

What does monthly bookkeeping cost?

Pricing depends on transaction volume, account complexity, reporting needs, and whether you want job-costing or advisory services. Contact JNB for a scope-specific quote.
